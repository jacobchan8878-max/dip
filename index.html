The deep end.

Since 2021, more than 200 students have entered over 150 competitions, from Korea and the Philippines to Jakarta, Greece and New York.

  • SEACSO International Round, Philippines: 5 special, 4 diamond, 1 gold and 2 silver awards
  • IWC & I³C, Jakarta: 2 golds for a biodegradable wood substitute
  • Young Inventors Challenge: 1 gold, 2 bronze
  • Jazro Cup Southern Zone: Robot Combat champions
  • AI MUDA@NADI Hackathon: national runner-up out of 26 finalist teams

  • SEACSO International Round, Korea: champion with a perfect score
  • Horizon Olympiad Global Round, New York: gold
  • Forward College Hackathon: champion and first runner-up
  • ISTEC: gold in Applied Technology
  • FIRST Global Challenge, Greece: three awards with Team Malaysia

  • SEACSO, Vietnam: 2 gold and 2 silver among the medals
  • National Scratch Olympiad: 1st, 2nd, 3rd and 5th place
  • Konvensyen Inovasi Besut: silver for a teen health app

  • SEACSO: a diamond medal and Top Scorer
  • International Scratch Olympiad: 7th place
